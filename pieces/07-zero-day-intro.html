Synthesising the soundtrack

Zero day

A 64-second demoscene intro: a security scanner boots, flies through its own code, watches an exploit crawl into the firewall, slams a patch shut and sends greetings. Every pixel and every sound comes from this one file.

  • Soundtrack synthesised at load in an OfflineAudioContext: oscillators, filtered noise, a resonant acid bass and a convolution reverb whose impulse is seeded noise
  • One pattern sequencer drives both the audio graph and the shader uniforms, so kicks punch the camera and arpeggio notes become glints
  • Raw WebGL2 effects: a hexagonal tunnel of code, a raymarched kaleidoscopic fractal, copper bars and a sine scroller
  • Datamosh glitch from a ping-pong framebuffer chain replayed from each cut, so every frame stays a pure function of time
  • Glyph atlases rasterised from Martian Mono at runtime; no images and no audio files
