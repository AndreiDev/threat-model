Loading Rapier physics (WebAssembly)

Transitive

Your app is a tower of other people's code. A hijacked patch release of one tiny package pulls it down, the recorded physics plays backwards, and a pinned lockfile keeps the tower standing.

  • Rapier 0.21 rigid bodies (Rust compiled to WebAssembly), stepped at a fixed 1/120 s from one fixed starting state
  • Every body's position and quaternion is recorded into a Float32Array at load, so playback, the slow-motion ramp, seeking and the rewind are all lookups with slerp between steps
  • Determinism is checked at load: the collapse is simulated twice and compared bit for bit
  • Three.js rounded-box blocks with canvas-drawn package labels, PCF soft shadows, an ironbow emissive shader and bloom
  • A rewind pass in the final shader: scanlines, chromatic offset and a cold tint
