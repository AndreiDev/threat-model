Thirty-six views of the firewall

Four woodblock prints after Hokusai, each printing itself block by block: the mountain is a stepped firewall around a datacentre, the sea is network traffic, and vermilion marks only the hostile packets that break against it.

  • Four Three.js scenes drawn into 11 ink separations with multiple render targets, then printed on procedural washi with per-block misregistration, woodgrain and bokashi
  • Two-tone cel lighting with noisy band thresholds and inverted-hull key lines; the great wave is a slab painted from a signed distance field
  • Ink-wash shadows: the inverted shadow map is smoothstepped, broken with world-space noise and edged with a separate noisy contour
  • Foam claws, spray and mackerel clouds made of thousands of instanced packet glyphs, outlined as one mass by a key-block pass
  • The transition is the printing sequence: key block, each colour block, then the seal; every frame is a pure function of t
