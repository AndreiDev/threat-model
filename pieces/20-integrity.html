Integrity

Every glyph here is this page's own source, rebuilt from inside itself and lit through its SHA-256 and SHA-384 fingerprints.

  • The script is a quine: it fills a hole in its own template with a quoted copy of that template, rebuilding this file byte for byte.
  • Subresource Integrity refuses a script whose bytes don't match its integrity hash. crypto.subtle.digest computes this page's own.
  • Flip one input bit and about half the output bits change. That is the avalanche effect, shown bit by bit.
  • Canvas 2D lights a mask of every glyph through the rings. Hover to read; click a glyph to flip a bit.
