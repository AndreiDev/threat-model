Sandbox

A falling-sand game where every material is a security idea. Malware eats data, ransomware locks it into crystal, and patches, backups and honeypots fight back. Draw anywhere to play.

Your browser has no WebGL2, so this is the plain canvas version without glow, shimmer and heat haze.

  • Cellular automaton on a 320 × 200 grid of typed arrays, scanned bottom-up in alternating directions with a per-cell moved-this-tick stamp
  • Powders pile, liquids level, gases rise; fifteen contact reactions all roll dice on one seeded xorshift stream
  • Cells upload every frame to a WebGL2 texture; shaders add per-grain shimmer, emissive glow, a two-level bloom and heat haze
  • The 30 s demo is scripted brush strokes on a fixed 60 Hz tick; seeking re-simulates from the seed, with snapshots every 2 s
  • Keys 1–9, 0 and E pick a material, [ and ] size the brush, right-click erases
