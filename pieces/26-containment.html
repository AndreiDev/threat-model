Containment

A cracked database leaks hot data into a containment tank. Stir, pour, tilt or shake it: agitation keeps it hot, and left alone it cools to ice.

  • MLS-MPM fluid (Hu et al. 2018) stepped by hand-written WGSL compute shaders
  • Particle-to-grid scatter with fixed-point atomicAdd, so every run and every seek is bit-identical
  • Screen-space fluid rendering: sphere depth, bilateral filter, normals from depth, thickness for absorption
  • Per-particle temperature: speed heats it, stillness cools it, the grid mixes it
