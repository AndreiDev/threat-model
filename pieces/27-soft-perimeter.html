Hanging the curtain

Soft perimeter

A firewall curtain hangs across a doorway while hostile packets blow against it from outside. Grab it and pull hard, or slash it, and they pour through the tear; Patch stitches it shut.

  • A hand-written XPBD cloth solver (Macklin et al. 2016 and 2019): 121 × 84 particles, about 60,000 distance constraints for stretch, shear and bending, 10 substeps of one Gauss–Seidel pass each per 1/60 s frame, in plain JS typed arrays
  • Tearing changes the mesh: when the weave is stretched across a grid line past its limit, the vertices on that line are split so the hole really opens, cracks grow from their tips, and Patch merges the vertices back
  • Packets are particles tested against the cloth through a spatial hash; the ones that get through warm a heat map on the floor
  • Three.js r186 MeshPhysicalMaterial with sheen over a procedural twill of code glyphs (canvas texture plus a derived normal map), frayed edges in the shader, two shadowed lights and bloom
  • The attract mode is a scripted session that is re-simulated from a fixed seed when you seek

Keys: R reset, C cut tool, T tear, P patch, Space pause, H notes.
