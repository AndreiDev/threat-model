Hands on

A defence game for bare hands: pinch hot threats before they reach the core, flick them into the cold field, and raise a palm to shatter volleys.

  • MediaPipe Tasks Vision 1.0.1 HandLandmarker (float16, GPU with CPU fallback): 21 landmarks per hand, on device
  • One Euro filtered and tuned at 30 detections/s; pinch, palm, fist and flick classified with hysteresis and debounce
  • Three.js 0.186 with bloom: hand skeletons, fingertip trails, a hex shield with heat-ring impacts
  • The demo hand is scripted landmark data through the same classifier at a fixed 60 Hz, so frames re-simulate exactly
  • Mouse and touch drive a virtual hand; nothing downloads until you click the camera button
